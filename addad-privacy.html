1. What the app collects

2. Why we collect it

  • Account identifiers — so we can recognise you and restore your data on a new device.
  • App content — this is the service itself: keeping your counters synced.
  • Technical data — to diagnose crashes and fix faults.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use your counter content to build advertising profiles.

3. Legal basis

Account identifiers and app content are processed because they are necessary to perform the contract between you and us — the app cannot sync without them. Crash and diagnostic data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, and on your consent where the platform asks for it.

5. Where data is stored

Your data is stored on servers located in country or region. Where personal data is transferred outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, we do so under the conditions permitted by the Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations.

6. How long we keep it

  • While your account is active: for as long as you keep using Addad.
  • After you delete your account: account identifiers and counter content are erased within e.g. 30 days.
  • Backups: residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for up to e.g. 90 days before being overwritten.
  • Crash logs: retained for e.g. 90 days, then deleted.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. You do not need to contact us first, and you do not need to have the app installed.

From inside the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Deletion begins immediately.

Without the app

Send a deletion request from the email address on your account to naif@rqn.sa with the subject Delete my Addad account. We will confirm within e.g. 7 days and complete the erasure within e.g. 30 days.

What is deleted: your account identifiers, all counters, their values and history. What may remain: anonymised, non-identifying records that cannot be linked back to you, and any records we are required by law to retain.

This section is a Google Play requirement. Apps that let users create an account must offer deletion from inside the app and provide a publicly reachable web route to request it. The URL of this page plus #delete is what goes into the Play Console data-deletion field.

8. Children

Addad is not directed at children under age you declare in the stores, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access restricted to the staff who need it to operate the service. Passwords, where we hold them, are stored hashed and are never readable by us. No system is completely secure, but we take measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. If a breach occurs that is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the competent authority as the regulations require.

10. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you may:

  • Be informed of why and on what basis your data is processed.
  • Access the data held about you.
  • Request a copy in a readable, portable format.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion — see section 7.
  • Withdraw consent, where processing rests on consent.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you may complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
