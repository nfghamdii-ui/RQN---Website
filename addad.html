Everything covering the Addad vehicle maintenance app: how your data is handled, the terms you agree to, and answers to common questions.

Last updated: 02 / 08 / 2026

1. What the app collects

CategoryWhat it includes
AccountEmail address and sign-in credentials — used to create your account and sync your data
What you enterVehicle name, odometer readings, service records and their costs, fuel fill-ups, and reminders

The app does not collect your location or your contacts, and we run no analytics or crash-reporting tool of our own. Nothing is collected beyond what the app needs to work.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app's features: reminders, records, reports, and sync.
  • To contact you about your account or a material update.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not serve advertising built on it. It is disclosed only to the technical providers necessary to operate the service, or where a legal obligation requires it.

3. Legal basis

Account data and the records you enter are processed because they are necessary to perform the contract between you and us — the app cannot sync without them and your records would be lost when you change device.

4. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a cloud provider, tied to your account alone, with access controls that prevent any other user from reading it and encryption in transit. Cloud sync is what keeps your records with you when you change device.

Location of servers: your data sits on Supabase infrastructure, which may be located outside the Kingdom. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we do so under the conditions permitted by the Personal Data Protection Law.

6. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists: we keep your data so the service works.
  • After deletion: your data is erased with the account within 30 days, except what we must retain by law.

7. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and everything in it at any time. You do not need our permission, and you do not need the app installed.

From inside the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Deletion begins immediately.

Without the app

Send a request from the email address on your account to naif@rqn.sa with the subject Delete my Addad account. We confirm within 7 days and complete the erasure within 30 days.

What is deleted: your account, vehicles, odometer readings, service and fuel records, and reminders. What may remain: anonymised records that cannot be linked to you, and anything the law requires us to keep.

8. Children

Addad is aimed at vehicle owners and is not designed for anyone below driving age. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your rights

Everything you enter is visible inside the app and editable at any time. Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you may also:

  • Be informed of the purpose and legal basis of processing.
  • Access your data and request a copy in a readable format.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion — see section 7.
  • Withdraw consent where processing rests on it.

Write to us and we will respond within the period set by the regulations. If our response does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

Frequently asked

How do I set an oil reminder by both distance and time?

When you create the reminder, turn on every distance and enter the kilometres, then turn on every period and choose the duration. The app alerts you on whichever falls due first — which is how manufacturers' schedules are written.

Is my data safe if I change my phone?

Yes. Your data is tied to your account and synced to the cloud. Sign in on any device and you will find all of it there.

How is fuel consumption calculated?

From the difference in odometer readings between two consecutive fill-ups against the litres added. So entering the odometer accurately with every fill-up is what makes the figure accurate.

Should I follow the suggested reminders exactly?

They are commonly used indicative intervals. Your vehicle's owner manual is the final reference, and you can adjust any reminder to match your dealer's recommendation.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, open Settings → Delete account to remove everything permanently. You can also request it by email without the app — see the deletion section in the privacy tab.

How do I cancel my subscription or request a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google directly.

A reminder did not arrive. What should I check?

Make sure notifications are enabled for Addad in your phone's settings, that battery saving is not restricting the app in the background, and that you are signed in to your account.
