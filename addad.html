Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set an oil change reminder by distance and time?

When creating a reminder, enable "every distance" and enter the kilometres, then enable "every period" and set the duration. The app will alert you on whichever comes first, matching official maintenance manuals.

Will I lose my data if I change my phone?

Not at all. Your data is safely synced to the cloud and tied to your account. Simply sign in on your new device to access all your records.

How is fuel consumption calculated?

It is calculated using the odometer difference between two consecutive fill-ups against litres added. Entering your odometer reading with each fill-up ensures maximum accuracy.

Should I follow the default reminders strictly?

Suggested reminders are common general estimates. Your vehicle's manual is the primary reference, and you can easily adjust any reminder to match your dealer's recommendations.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete everything instantly in the app via Settings → Delete Account, or request deletion by email without needing the app; see the privacy tab.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & Subscriptions. Refunds are processed directly by Apple or Google.

I didn't receive a notification, how do I fix this?

Ensure notifications are allowed for Addad in phone settings, disable strict battery saver modes for the app, and verify you are signed into your account.
