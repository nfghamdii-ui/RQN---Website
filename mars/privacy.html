1. Using the app without an account

You can use MARS without an account. Your saved words, progress and settings then stay on your device only.

2. When you sign in

Signing in is optional (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). We then store:

  • The account identifier Apple or Google provides, and your email if you share it (Apple lets you hide it).
  • Your learning progress: saved words, their review schedule and daily counts.
  • Your in-app settings: level, daily goal, appearance and similar.

We use these only to keep your progress and sync it across your devices.

3. AI features

"Ask about it" and looking up a new word use Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. Only the text of your question and the word are sent, never your name, email or account ID, and we ask for your consent before your first question. Answers are stored with the word and question, not with you, so the same question can be answered again later. Please don't include personal information in your questions.

4. "Hear it from people" clips

Clips are shown via YouGlish and the YouTube player. When they play, YouTube may collect data under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

5. IP address

To protect the service from abuse, we log your IP address with the time of each AI or clip request, and delete it automatically after 7 days.

6. Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on your device and never passes through our servers.

7. Where data is stored

Our servers are hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom (London). Our providers process data on our behalf under their own agreements: Supabase (hosting), Anthropic (AI), Apple and Google (sign-in).

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. The account and its stored progress are deleted from our servers immediately. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

9. Children

MARS is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
