1. Who we are

RQN is a registered Saudi company working in IT services, network infrastructure, and security camera systems. Addad, an app for tracking vehicle maintenance, is one of our software products. For the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Law, RQN is the data controller for the data described here.

  • Legal name: Full registered company name
  • Commercial registration: CR number
  • Address: Street, district, Riyadh

2. What the Addad app collects

3. What the website collects

Browsing rqn.sa does not require an account. If you submit the site visit request form, we receive your name, mobile number, facility type, city, and any message you write. We use essential operational files and aggregated visit measurements only — no advertising trackers.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app's features: reminders, records, reports, and sync.
  • To improve the product and diagnose faults.
  • To contact you about your account or a material update.
  • To reply to enquiries submitted through the website and prepare quotations.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not serve advertising built on it. It is disclosed only to the technical providers necessary to operate the service, or where a legal obligation requires it.

5. Legal basis

Account data and the records you enter are processed because they are necessary to perform the contract between you and us — the app cannot sync without them. Crash and diagnostic data rests on our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable. Website enquiries rest on your consent and on steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

6. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a cloud provider, tied to your account alone, with access controls that prevent any other user from reading it and encryption in transit. Cloud sync is what keeps your records with you when you change device.

Location of servers: Name the Supabase region, e.g. Frankfurt (EU). Where personal data is transferred outside the Kingdom, we do so under the conditions permitted by the Personal Data Protection Law.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists: we keep your data so the service works.
  • After deletion: your data is erased with the account, within e.g. 30 days, except what we must retain by law.
  • Backups: residual encrypted copies may persist up to e.g. 90 days before being overwritten.

9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and everything in it at any time. You do not need our permission, and you do not need the app installed.

From inside the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Deletion begins immediately.

Without the app

Send a request from the email address on your account to naif@rqn.sa with the subject Delete my Addad account. We confirm within e.g. 7 days and complete the erasure within e.g. 30 days.

What is deleted: your account, vehicles, odometer readings, service and fuel records, and reminders. What may remain: anonymised records that cannot be linked to you, and anything the law requires us to keep.

Store note: Google Play requires apps with account creation to offer deletion inside the app and a publicly reachable web route. Paste https://rqn.sa/privacy#delete into the Play Console data-deletion field.

10. Children

Addad is aimed at vehicle owners and is not designed for anyone below driving age. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Your rights

Everything you enter is visible inside the app and editable at any time. Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you may also:

  • Be informed of the purpose and legal basis of processing.
  • Access your data and request a copy in a readable format.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion — see section 9.
  • Withdraw consent where processing rests on it.

Write to us and we will respond within the period set by the regulations. If our response does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
