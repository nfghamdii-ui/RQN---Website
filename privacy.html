1. Who we are

RQN is a registered Saudi company working in IT services, network infrastructure, and security camera systems, operating in Jeddah and Makkah. For the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Law, RQN is the data controller for the information described on this page.

  • Legal name: Full registered company name
  • Commercial registration: CR number
  • Address: Street, district, Jeddah

2. What this website collects

Browsing rqn.sa requires no account and no sign-in. We collect information in two situations only.

SituationWhat we receive
You submit the site visit formYour name, mobile number, facility type, city, and anything you write in the message field
You simply browseEssential operational files and aggregated visit measurements that do not identify you

We use no advertising trackers and we do not build a profile of you across other websites.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange the site visit.
  • To prepare a technical proposal or a quotation for you.
  • To keep a record of the work if you become a client.
  • To understand which parts of the site people read, using aggregated figures.

We do not sell your data and we do not pass it to advertisers. We do not send marketing messages to a number you gave us for a site visit unless you ask us to.

4. Legal basis

Form submissions are processed on the basis of your consent and of steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. Aggregated site measurement rests on our legitimate interest in maintaining the website. Where a client relationship begins, contract performance and our statutory record-keeping obligations apply.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that go nowhere: deleted within e.g. 12 months.
  • Client records: kept for the period our commercial and tax obligations require.
  • Site measurements: aggregated and not linked to you as an individual.

7. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you may:

  • Be informed of the purpose and legal basis of processing.
  • Access your data and request a copy in a readable format.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion of what we hold, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent where processing rests on it.

Write to us and we will respond within the period set by the regulations. If our response does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
