3. Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a site visit.
  • To prepare a written quotation for the work you described.
  • To keep a record of quotations issued, for accounting and warranty purposes.

We do not use your details for marketing campaigns, and we do not sell or rent them to anyone.

4. Legal basis

We process this data on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you submit the form, and on the basis that processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. You may withdraw consent at any time — see section 9.

5. Who we share it with

We share your data only where it is necessary to answer you or where the law requires it:

  • Our hosting and form provider: Name the service that receives form submissions
  • Competent authorities, where disclosure is required by law or a valid order.

Transfers outside the Kingdom: State whether the provider stores data outside Saudi Arabia, and in which country. Where data leaves the Kingdom, we do so under the conditions permitted by the Personal Data Protection Law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: e.g. 12 months, then deleted.
  • Enquiries that become projects: retained for the life of the contract and the statutory record-keeping period that follows.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Your language choice is held in the page only and is forgotten when you close the tab.

If analytics is added to the site later, this section must be updated before it goes live, and a consent banner may be required.

8. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to submitted enquiries is limited to RQN staff who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

9. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

  • Be informed of the legal basis and purpose of collection.
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request a copy of it in a readable format.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion where the data is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the period set by the regulations. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA), the supervisory authority for data protection in the Kingdom.
